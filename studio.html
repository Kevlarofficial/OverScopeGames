DOC.01

Solo, by design

Over Scope Games is a solo indie studio — one person handling design, development, and everything in between.

The name is the mission statement. Every project here is deliberately built past the edge of what's "reasonable" for a one-person team: interconnected systems, custom architecture, and mechanics that most solo devs would scope down or cut entirely. That's the standard here, not the exception.

None of that happens in isolation. Community feedback is factored directly into development decisions, from early concept through iteration — the people who'll actually play these games have a hand in shaping them.

DOC.02

Why over-scope

Most solo dev advice says the same thing: cut scope, ship small, avoid ambition you can't sustain alone. That advice is correct for most projects — and deliberately not the approach here. The bet is that a handful of deep, technically difficult systems, done properly, matter more than a large number of shallow ones. That means longer builds, harder debugging, and more false starts. It also means the finished systems hold up.
